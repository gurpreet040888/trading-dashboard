How to read this: everything here — value trends, quantity trends, and each holding's XIRR — is computed only from your statement snapshots (SOH.txt), never from a live market feed. A quantity increase is treated as a purchase at that statement's price, a decrease as a sale, and clean multiples (2×–10×) paired with an inverse price move are treated as a split/bonus rather than a purchase. Holdings with only one or two recent snapshots can show extreme-looking XIRR — a small price move over a short holding period gets annualized into a very large percentage, so treat those numbers as noisy until more history accumulates. "Hold / Review / Watch" labels are rule-based prompts to help you decide what to look into — not investment advice, and not a substitute for a licensed advisor.
