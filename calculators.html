Year Age Phase Interest earned (₹) Pension withdrawn (₹) Remaining balance (₹)
From your current age to the pension start age, the corpus simply compounds — no withdrawals. From the start age onward, each month interest accrues first, then the monthly pension is withdrawn. A row is flagged if the corpus runs out before the tenure ends.
Month EMI (₹) Interest paid (₹) Principal repaid (₹) Loan balance (₹) Investment value (₹) Net position (₹)
Assumes you buy the item either way — the only choice is whether you pay cash upfront, or take the loan and keep your cash invested. "Loan balance" is the reducing EMI-loan outstanding; "Investment value" is the invested amount compounding at the investment rate; "Net position" = Investment value − Loan balance, i.e. what you'd be left with if you settled the loan from the investment right then. This ignores taxes on loan interest deductions and on investment gains.
